About Knowsies

Knowsies is a free “How well do you know me?” quiz maker. Answer ten questions about yourself, share one link to your story or group chat, and see which friend knows you best.

It is built to be fast and clean on a phone, including inside Instagram, Snapchat and WhatsApp. There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking, and every quiz deletes itself after 90 days.
