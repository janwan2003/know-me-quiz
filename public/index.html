Questions people ask

Do my friends need an app or an account?

No. They open the link, type a nickname and play in their browser, including inside Instagram, Snapchat and WhatsApp.

Can my friends see the answers before they play?

No. Each answer is revealed only after they lock in their guess, so nobody can peek.

What do you store?

Your first name, your questions and answers, and each friend's nickname and score. No email, no phone number, no tracking. Every quiz is deleted 90 days after it was made, or straight away when you delete it.

How do I see my results later?

Keep the creator link we show you after you make the quiz. This browser also remembers your quizzes on the home page.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads between questions, no paywall on results.

Someone made a mean quiz. What can I do?

Tap “Report this quiz” at the bottom of the quiz page. Quizzes with several reports are taken down automatically. Names and custom questions are also filtered for insults.
