Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Knowsies lets you make a “How well do you know me?” quiz and share it with friends. We collect only what the quiz needs to work.

What we store

  • Quiz creators: the first name you type, your questions and your answers, and a fingerprint (hash) of your private creator key.
  • People taking a quiz: the nickname you type, the answers you pick and your score, and a hash of your private play key.
  • Abuse protection: a short-lived counter per network address (IP) to stop one visitor from flooding the service. Counters expire within an hour, except a per-quiz marker that stops the same network reporting a quiz twice, which expires with the quiz.

We do not ask for an email address, phone number or account. We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, advertising or third-party scripts. Your browser keeps your private keys in its local storage so you can come back to your quiz or your score.

Who can see what

Anyone with the quiz link can see the creator’s first name, the questions, and the leaderboard of nicknames and scores. The correct answers are shown to a player only after they answer each question. Only the creator (with the private creator link) can see which answer each player picked, remove players and delete the quiz.

How long we keep it

Every quiz is deleted, together with all of its scores, 90 days after it was created. A creator can delete it sooner with “Delete quiz now”. Quizzes reported by several people are hidden automatically.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages and D1), which hosts the service.

Children

Knowsies is meant for people aged 13 and over. Please don’t put personal details such as addresses, phone numbers or school names in a quiz.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: open an issue at github.com/janwan2003/know-me-quiz/issues, or use “Report this quiz” on the quiz page.
